# Beat Commodus Event Plan

## Summary

Run a one-day launch challenge on Monday, April 27, 2026.

Players try to outscore Commodus on the leaderboard before the end of the day. The message is simple:

- fund
- trade
- score
- pass Commodus
- share it

The event should make Victus feel like a Farcaster-native game, not just a Mini App that happens to live inside Farcaster.

## Primary Goal

Create a public loop that gets players into the Mini App and back into the feed:

1. Open Victus.
2. Fund wallet.
3. Make a trade.
4. Check Standings.
5. Share rank or a "Beat Commodus" moment.
6. Pull in the next player.

## Event Window

Date: Monday, April 27, 2026

Recommended window: 12:00 PM to 11:59 PM Eastern.

This leaves the morning for final prep, gives the event a clear afternoon start, and creates an evening leaderboard push.

## Core Messaging

Use short, direct copy:

- The arena opens Monday.
- Beat Commodus.
- Fund once. Trade publicly. Climb the board.
- Pass the emperor before the day ends.
- The leaderboard is the receipt.

Do not frame the event as financial advice. It is a public trading game and leaderboard challenge.

## Mini App Priorities Before Monday

1. Make sure Standings clearly shows Commodus as the boss benchmark.
2. Make sure the wallet funding path works cleanly from a fresh user.
3. Replace any remaining notification or template copy that says "Farcaster Mini App Template."
4. Add or manually prepare share copy for:
   - funded wallet
   - first trade
   - current rank
   - passed Commodus
5. Confirm `NEXT_PUBLIC_URL` points to the live app URL and feed previews render correctly.

## Launch Casts

### Morning Announcement

```text
The arena opens today.
Fund your wallet, make your trade, and try to pass Commodus before midnight.
The leaderboard is live.
```

### Midday Push

```text
Commodus is on the board.
You have until tonight to pass him.
Open Victus. Trade. Score. Make it public.
```

### Evening Urgency

```text
Final hours.
If you are below Commodus, this is the part where you stop pretending it was strategy.
```

## In-App Event Framing

Standings should emphasize:

- Commodus score
- viewer score
- points needed to pass Commodus
- current rank
- remaining daily trades

Primary CTA: `Trade Now`, linking to `?tab=trade`.

## Success Metrics

Track manually or from existing analytics:

- Mini App opens on April 27
- new sign-ins
- wallets provisioned
- wallets funded
- trades placed
- unique traders
- casts mentioning Commodus or Victus
- number of users who pass Commodus
- final top 10 standings

## Day-After Follow-Up

On Tuesday, April 28, 2026:

- cast the top 10
- call out everyone who beat Commodus
- share one or two standout trades
- announce the next arena window or monthly leaderboard continuation
